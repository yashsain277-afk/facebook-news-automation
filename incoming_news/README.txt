VEE NEWS - INCOMING NEWS

Put ONE .txt or .md file plus ONE photo or video in this folder.

Example:
- news.txt
- photo.jpg

OR:
- news.txt
- footage.mp4

After you commit/upload both files, the Vee News workflow automatically:
1. reads the news text,
2. converts it into a short Hindi news script,
3. creates a 30-55 second vertical video,
4. adds Hindi voice-over and Vee News graphics,
5. uploads the finished video to Facebook.

If both photo and video are present, the newest media file is used.

Do not upload passwords, access tokens, or app secrets here.
